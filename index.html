SUNDAY AT 6: SOUND OFF FOR LIBERTY AND JUSTICE

Sunday March 15 at 6 PM local time, for 60 seconds, let's turn up the volume to declare support for fundamental American values and to denounce the Trump administration’s authoritarian actions, incendiary scapegoating, and deadly injustices.

Silence won’t beat tyrants, so go outside on your block or campus and turn up the volume Sunday at 6. Together with neighbors we'll raise an emergency alert for our country.

We'll bang on pots or blow on whistles or honk horns.

For one minute. Four times the length of the Pledge of Allegiance. Twice the length of the Final Jeopardy theme. For one minute, we'll exercise our basic American freedom to sound off!

Because America is supposed to be a democracy, not a dictatorship!

Sunday at 6, let's raise the roof with our collective call for liberty and justice.

MORE SHARES = MORE SOUND ON SUNDAY

Sunday at 6 is an idea that sprung from the grassroots. If you like the sound of it, share it! Spread the word to your neighbors, friends, relatives. Talk, text, post!

THE SOUND OF SOLIDARITY

The sound of spoons banging on pots is heard in protests around the world. In many countries, the percussive protest is called a cacerolazo. In Canada, it’s a casserole. Sunday at 6, let's make one hell of a casserole!
