SUNDAY AT 6: SOUND OFF FOR LIBERTY AND JUSTICE

Sunday March 1 at 6 PM local time, for 60 seconds, neighbors all across our country will turn up the volume to declare our support for fundamental American values and to denounce the current administration’s authoritarian actions, incendiary scapegoating, and deadly injustices.

What this administration is doing is a modern-day version of the sort of tyranny that Americans in the “Greatest Generation” heroically defeated overseas.  

Silence won’t beat tyrants, so we urge you to join neighbors on your block who are turning up the volume. Together we'll raise an emergency alert for our country. We’ll go outside and bang on pots or blow on whistles or honk horns.

Sunday at 6, we’ll make the largest, loudest collective call for liberty and justice in the history of our nation.

Because America is supposed to be a democracy, not a dictatorship!

THE SOUND OF SOLIDARITY

The sound of spoons banging on pots is heard in protests around the world. In South America, the percussive protest is called a caceroloza.  In Canada, it’s a casserole.   Sunday at 6, we're going to make one hell of a casserole!
